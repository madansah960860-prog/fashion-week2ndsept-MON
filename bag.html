What happens at checkout

Payment

  • Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay
  • Card data goes directly to our payment processor over TLS. It never touches our servers
  • Gift cards and referral credit can be combined with any payment method

Taxes and duties

  • US sales tax is calculated at checkout from your shipping address
  • Oregon has no sales tax, but we collect where we have an obligation to
  • International orders are shipped without US tax, and duties are paid by the recipient
  • Nothing is added after you confirm the order

After you order

  • An order confirmation arrives immediately with an order number
  • A tracking number follows within one business day
  • Bench Made pieces show an estimated ship date, currently 18 to 24 business days
  • You can change or cancel any order that has not shipped by emailing us

No dark patterns at checkout

Nothing is pre-added to your bag, there is no pre-ticked subscription box, no artificial timer holding your items, and no fee that appears only on the final screen. If the total changes between this page and the payment step, the only reason is shipping selection and sales tax, both shown before you pay.
