Wholesale, published openly

We publish our wholesale terms rather than negotiating them case by case, because the shops we want to work with are small and do not have time for a three-week back and forth about margin.

Terms

  • Opening order minimum: $2,500 at wholesale, any mix of styles
  • Reorder minimum: $750
  • Margin: Keystone, 50% off the published retail price
  • Payment: Net 30 after the first order, prepay on the first
  • Lead time: 10 to 15 business days from purchase order for core styles
  • Drops: Wholesale allocation for capsule drops closes eight weeks before the release date

What we ask of partners

  • A physical store. We do not supply online-only resellers or marketplaces
  • Retail pricing held at our published US price, so customers are not penalised for buying locally
  • Staff willing to learn the fabric detail. We provide a training sheet and samples
  • No listing on third-party marketplace platforms

What partners get

  • Free shipping on orders over the reorder minimum
  • Swap privileges on unsold core stock, twice a year
  • Damaged or faulty stock replaced without a claim process
  • Your store listed on this page and named in our customer support replies
