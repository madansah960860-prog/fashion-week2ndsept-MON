Shipping and delivery

Every order ships from our own warehouse on SE Water Avenue in Portland, Oregon. Nothing is drop-shipped, so when you email us about a parcel, we can go and look at the shelf it came from.

Processing and cut-off

  • Orders placed before 1:00 p.m. Pacific on a business day ship the same day
  • Everything else ships the next business day
  • We do not ship on weekends or US federal holidays
  • Bench Made made-to-order pieces ship after an 18 to 24 business day production window

Tracking

  • A tracking number is emailed as soon as the label is created
  • Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show movement, which is normal
  • Split shipments are not charged twice and are tracked separately
  • No account is required to track an order
Free shipping threshold$125

The threshold is set at roughly our average parcel cost. It is not a psychological number designed to push your order size up.

What we cannot do for international orders

  • We cannot estimate your duties. They vary by country and by how your customs authority classifies the goods
  • We cannot mark a parcel as a gift or understate its value. That is customs fraud and we will not do it
  • We cannot refund duties if you return an item. Your customs authority may allow you to reclaim them, and we will provide documentation to support that
  • We do not offer free international returns. Return shipping from outside the US is at your cost

When something goes wrong

Marked delivered, not received

  • Check side doors, porches and with neighbours first, most turn up within 48 hours
  • If it has not appeared after two business days, email us
  • We send a replacement and handle the carrier claim ourselves
  • We do not require a police report or an affidavit

Damaged in transit

  • Email a photo of the parcel and the item
  • Replacement ships the same or next business day
  • You do not need to return the damaged item before we send the replacement
  • No cost to you in any circumstance

Wrong item or wrong size sent

  • Our error, so we cover shipping in both directions
  • We hold the correct item for you while the wrong one is in transit
  • Email us and we will send a prepaid label the same day
  • Refunds, if you would rather not wait, are processed within two business days

Address changes

Email us before the parcel ships and we will change it at no cost. Once a tracking number exists, the carrier controls it, and re-routing is unreliable. If you spot an address error immediately after ordering, write to us straight away rather than waiting.

Packaging

  • Outbound parcels use paper mailers and cartons, both recyclable and made from recycled content
  • No plastic polybags in outbound shipping
  • Inbound stock from our factories still arrives in polybags. We reuse them internally where we can, and we are working on it
  • No printed catalogs or inserts unless you ask for a line sheet
