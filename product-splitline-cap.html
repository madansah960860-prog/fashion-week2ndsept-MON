Accessories, core line

Splitline Running Cap

$42

A five-panel cap with a soft brim you can fold into a jacket pocket and unfold without a permanent crease. Laser-cut vents run through both side panels, and the sweatband is a brushed knit that actually holds moisture rather than pushing it into your eyes.

The rear panel is elastic rather than a plastic snap, so there is no hard edge under a hood and nothing to catch on sunglasses arms.

Fabric

  • Crown and brim: 100% recycled polyester ripstop, 40 denier
  • Vents: Laser-cut, no mesh insert to trap grit
  • Sweatband: Brushed polyester knit with a silicone gripper strip
  • Brim core: Flexible EVA, folds and recovers
  • Weight: 1.9 oz (54 g)

Fit

  • Size: One size, elastic rear panel fits 21 to 24 in head circumference
  • Brim: 2.75 in, angled slightly down to shade low sun
  • Profile: Low crown, sits close rather than tall
  • Model: 22.5 in head circumference, no adjustment needed

Care

  • Wash: Cold hand wash or cold gentle cycle in a mesh bag
  • Dry: Air dry on a shaped surface, never tumble
  • Avoid: Bleach, which yellows the reflective trim
  • Salt: Rinse after hot sessions to stop salt rings forming on the brim

Sizing

  • Range: One size only, no S or L split
  • How to measure: Tape around the head just above the ears and eyebrows
  • Above 24 in: Email us. We can advise on the fit before you order
  • Full chart: Size guide, accessories section

The rest of the detail

A stiff brim holds its shape better on a shelf, but it cannot be folded into a pocket, and it snaps if you sit on it. Our brim uses a flexible EVA core that recovers its shape after being packed. The trade-off is honest: in strong headwind the brim flexes slightly more than a rigid one.

Black and volt are core colorways and stay in stock year-round. Capsule colorways arrive with each drop and are not restocked once they sell through. Drop 07 opens on October 2, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Pacific.
