Marginal gains, worn

A tenth of a second is a material decision.

We started Splitsec because training kit kept failing in the same four places: the zip garage, the underarm seam, the waistband elastic and the print. So we build in the opposite order. Fabric first, seam construction second, fit third, graphics last, and only if the graphic survives fifty washes.

Every garment gets a wear-test block before it goes on sale: eight weeks, twelve athletes, mixed climates from Portland rain to Phoenix heat. If a sample comes back with a failure, the run does not open. That is why our line moves slowly and why the core does not change with the season.

Product spotlight

Velocity Shell Jacket

$268

A 2.5-layer running shell with a 20,000mm waterproof rating and taped seams, cut long at the back so it stays put over a pack. Packs into its own chest pocket at roughly the size of a water bottle.

Fabric

  • Shell: 100% recycled ripstop nylon, 40 denier, bluesign approved
  • Membrane: PFAS-free polyurethane, 20,000mm water column, 15,000g breathability
  • Weight: 8.4 oz in a men's medium
  • Finish: PFAS-free durable water repellent, renewable with low heat tumble

Fit

  • Cut: Athletic regular, room for a mid layer without flap
  • Length: Dropped back hem, 2 in longer than the front
  • Sleeve: Articulated elbow, elastic-bound cuff
  • Model: 6 ft 1 in, chest 40 in, wearing size medium

Care and sizing

  • Wash: Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle with technical wash
  • Dry: Tumble low for 20 minutes to reactivate the water repellent
  • Avoid: Fabric softener, bleach, dry cleaning, direct iron heat
  • Sizing: XS to XXL. Between sizes, take the smaller size for racing and the larger for layering

How Splitsec actually works

We sell direct, in US dollars, from our own site. No wholesale markup baked into the price, no seasonal markdown cycle, no invented urgency.

Seasonless core plus dated drops

  • Twenty-two core styles stay in stock year-round and get restocked, not replaced
  • Two capsule drops per year in small runs. Drop 06 released on March 6, 2026
  • Drop 07 opens on October 2, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Pacific
  • When a run is gone we mark it sold through instead of leaving a dead listing up

Shipping and returns

  • Free US standard shipping over $125, flat $8 below that threshold
  • Two to five business days in the contiguous US, from Portland, Oregon
  • 30 days to return or exchange, unworn with tags attached
  • Exchange shipping is on us. Refund returns carry a $7 label fee

Made to order, honestly timed

  • The Bench Made line is cut after you order, in Portland
  • Current lead time is 18 to 24 business days, shown on each product page
  • You are charged at order because the fabric is cut for you
  • Bench Made pieces can be exchanged for size but are not eligible for refund

Gift cards, referrals, wholesale

  • Digital gift cards from $25 to $500, no expiry, no fees
  • Referral program gives your friend $20 and gives you $20 after their first order ships
