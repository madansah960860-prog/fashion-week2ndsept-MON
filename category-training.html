The kit that repeats

These are the pieces you own two or three of. Inseams, waistband construction and liner details are all listed, because those are what decide whether a short works for you.

Kilometre Short 5 in

$76

A five inch short with a bonded liner and a waistband wide enough to hold a phone without sagging. The split hem gives full stride clearance without riding up.

Fabric

  • Shell: 100% recycled polyester woven, 85 gsm, with a PFAS-free repellent finish
  • Liner: 82% recycled polyester, 18% elastane brief liner
  • Weight: 3.6 oz in men's medium

Fit and construction

  • Inseam: 5 in across all sizes
  • Waistband: 2 in bonded band, internal drawcord, two zip pockets
  • Model: 5 ft 10 in, 32 in waist, wears size M

Care and sizing

  • Wash: Cold, gentle, rinse salt out after hot sessions
  • Dry: Line dry, tumble low only if needed
  • Sizing: XS to XXL. Waist runs true, the liner runs slightly snug

Split Tights

$118

Full-length tights with a brushed inner face for winter and a 6 in ankle zip so they clear a shoe without a fight. Two hip pockets and one zip pocket at the back.

Fabric

  • Body: 76% recycled polyester, 24% elastane, brushed inside, 240 gsm
  • Compression: Moderate, supportive without restricting stride
  • Weight: 7.9 oz in men's medium

Fit

  • Inseam: 28 in XS to S, 29.5 in M to L, 31 in XL to XXL

  • Model: 5 ft 6 in, 28 in waist, wears size XS

Care and sizing

  • Wash: Cold, inside out, no softener because it coats elastane

  • Sizing: XS to XXL, true to size. Size up for a looser compression feel

Buying two of something

Most people who train four or more times a week end up with two pairs of shorts and two tees in rotation. That is the honest recommendation, not an upsell: one pair means washing on a schedule set by your laundry rather than your training.
