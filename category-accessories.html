Grip Crew Socks Three-Pack

$34

Three pairs, knit in one piece with a seamless toe and a compression arch band. The silicone grip lines on the sole keep them from twisting inside a training shoe.

Fabric

  • Body: 58% combed cotton, 38% recycled polyester, 4% elastane
  • Sole: Cushioned terry underfoot, flat everywhere else
  • Grip: Silicone print lines, latex free

Fit

  • Size S: US women's 5 to 7, US men's 4 to 6
  • Size M: US women's 7.5 to 10, US men's 6.5 to 9
  • Size L: US women's 10.5 to 13, US men's 9.5 to 12
  • Height: Crew, sits 4 in above the ankle bone

Care

  • Wash: Warm, with like colors
  • Dry: Tumble low, high heat degrades the elastane
  • Avoid: Bleach and fabric softener, both kill the grip print

Kinetic Duffel 32L

$178

Sized to hold a full change of kit, shoes and a laptop without becoming a suitcase. The wet compartment is a separate welded pocket, not a mesh divider that leaks.

Materials

  • Body: 100% recycled 600 denier polyester with a TPU backing
  • Base: Abrasion-resistant 1000 denier panel
  • Hardware: YKK zips, aluminum hook hardware
  • Weight: 1 lb 14 oz empty

Dimensions and layout

  • Size: 20 in long, 11 in tall, 10 in deep, 32 liter volume
  • Wet pocket: 8 liter welded compartment at one end
  • Laptop sleeve: Padded, fits up to 15 in
  • Carry: Grab handles plus a removable padded shoulder strap

Care and warranty

  • Clean: Wipe with a damp cloth, never machine wash
  • Dry: Air dry with the wet pocket open
  • Repair: Zip and strap repairs are free for the first three years
