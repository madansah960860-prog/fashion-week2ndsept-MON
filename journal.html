What this journal is for

Most brand blogs exist to catch search traffic. This one exists because our support inbox keeps asking the same four questions, and a product page is the wrong place to answer them at length.

Articles are published when there is something worth saying, which works out to roughly one every six weeks. They are written by our product developers and our two coaches, and they are edited for accuracy before style. Where an article recommends one of our products, it says so plainly, and it also says when something we sell is the wrong choice.

How we work

What we write about

  • Layering and kit selection: what to wear at what temperature and output level
  • Materials: what fabric claims mean, tested against what mills actually document
  • Training structure: block design, consistency, and the practical obstacles that end blocks early
  • Care and repair: getting more seasons out of technical apparel
  • What we got wrong: product decisions we reversed, and why

One email when a new article goes up, plus drop dates. No daily sends, no re-marketing sequences.
