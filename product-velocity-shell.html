Velocity Shell Jacket

$268

Our lightest fully waterproof shell, built for training in rain rather than sheltering from it. The membrane is rated to 20,000mm and every seam is taped, but the whole jacket still packs into its own chest pocket at roughly the size of a water bottle.

The back hem drops two inches below the front so it stays over a race belt when you lean into a hill, and the hood turns with your head instead of sliding across your face.

Fabric

  • Shell: 100% recycled ripstop nylon, 40 denier, bluesign approved mill
  • Membrane: PFAS-free polyurethane, 20,000mm water column, 15,000g/m2/24h breathability
  • Seams: Fully taped with 13mm seam tape
  • Finish: PFAS-free durable water repellent, renewable in a low tumble dry
  • Weight: 8.4 oz (238 g) in men's medium

Fit

  • Cut: Athletic regular. Fits over a base layer and a mid layer without pulling
  • Length: Dropped back hem, 2 in longer than the front
  • Sleeve: Articulated elbow, elastic bound cuff, no velcro tab
  • Hood: Adjustable at the rear, brim holds shape in wind
  • Model: 6 ft 1 in tall, 40 in chest, wearing size M

Care

  • Wash: Machine wash cold, gentle cycle, technical wash, zips closed
  • Dry: Tumble low for 20 minutes to reactivate the water repellent finish
  • Avoid: Fabric softener, bleach, dry cleaning, direct iron heat
  • Reproof: Spray-on treatment once a season once water stops beading

Sizing

  • Range: XS to XXL, unisex cut with US numeric equivalents in the size guide
  • Between sizes: Take the smaller size for racing, the larger for winter layering
  • Chest for size M: Fits 38 to 41 in body chest
  • Full chart: See the size guide in inches

The rest of the detail

  • Two-way front zip with an internal storm flap and a chin garage
  • One chest pocket, which doubles as the stuff sack. The jacket packs to roughly 8 in by 4 in
  • Laser-cut back vent under a bonded storm flap
  • Reflective trim at the rear hem and both cuffs
  • No internal hanging loop, because it adds a hard point that wears through the membrane

A 20,000mm rating means a 20,000 millimeter column of water can sit on the fabric before it leaks. In practice anything above 10,000mm handles sustained rain. The number that matters more for training is breathability: at 15,000g this shell moves enough vapor to run in without soaking from the inside on all but the warmest wet days.

If you mostly walk or commute in rain rather than run, the Rainshadow 2.5L is the better choice. It is heavier, longer and vents through pit zips.

We repair tears and failed zips on this jacket for as long as we can source the parts, at cost plus shipping. When it is genuinely finished, send it back and we will route it into textile recycling rather than let it go to landfill. Details on the sustainability page.
