Knitwear, core line

Thermal Grid Crew

$128

Grid fleece works because of what is missing. The knit leaves open channels against your skin, so warm air sits in the pockets while sweat travels along the grid lines and out. The result is a layer that stays warm while you work and dries in the time it takes to drive home.

This is the piece most of our customers own two of. It goes under the Velocity Shell in winter and works alone from about 45 degrees Fahrenheit upward.

Fabric

  • Body: 92% recycled polyester, 8% elastane grid fleece, 190 gsm
  • Odor control: Mineral treatment bonded into the yarn, not a surface coating that washes off
  • Cuffs and collar: Same fabric, bound rather than ribbed, so they do not stretch out
  • Weight: 9.2 oz (261 g) in men's medium

Fit

  • Cut: Slim. It is a mid layer, so it sits close enough to layer under a shell
  • Seams: Flatlock throughout, offset from the shoulder so a pack strap does not sit on them
  • Length: Standard body length, straight hem
  • Model: 5 ft 10 in tall, 38 in chest, wearing size M

Care

  • Wash: Machine wash cold, inside out, with like fabrics
  • Dry: Tumble low or line dry. Fleece dries fast, high heat is unnecessary
  • Avoid: Fabric softener, which fills the grid channels and kills the wicking
  • Pilling: Minimal on this knit. If it appears, a fabric comb removes it

Sizing

  • Range: XS to XXL with US numeric equivalents
  • Between sizes: Size up if you plan to wear it as an outer layer
  • Chest for size M: Fits 37 to 40 in body chest

The rest of the detail

Grid fleece beats merino when you are working hard and generating a lot of moisture, because it moves sweat faster and dries faster. Merino wins on multi-day wear, on low-output cold days, and when odor control over several sessions matters more than drying speed.

Our layering article works through the decision in detail, including where each fabric fails.

  • Flatlock seams offset from the shoulder line
  • Bound neck and cuffs, no ribbing to lose shape
  • Reflective heat transfer at the centre back, roughly 2 in wide
  • No zip pockets, because pocket bags in a mid layer add bulk under a shell
