Buying from us, plainly

Splitsec sells direct to you in US dollars. There is no wholesale layer inflating the price and no seasonal markdown calendar, so the number you see in September is the number you see in March.

Twenty-two of the thirty styles here are seasonless core pieces that get restocked rather than replaced. The rest belong to a dated capsule drop. Drop 06 released on March 6, 2026 and Drop 07 opens on October 2, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Pacific.

More about how we build

What every product page tells you

  • Full fabric composition by percentage, including trims
  • Garment weight in ounces and fabric weight in gsm
  • Fit notes plus the model's height and the size they wear
  • Care instructions written for technical fabric, not generic laundry advice
  • Body measurements in inches, cross-referenced to the size guide
