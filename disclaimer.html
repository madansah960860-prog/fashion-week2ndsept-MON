Disclaimer

Last updated September 1, 2026. This page explains how to read the numbers and advice published on this site, and states our position on advertising and affiliate arrangements.

Product performance figures

Figures such as waterproof ratings in millimeters, breathability in grams, fabric weights in gsm and garment weights in ounces come from test reports supplied by the mills that make our fabric, or from our own measurements of production samples.

  • Laboratory ratings describe controlled conditions. Real-world performance depends on temperature, humidity, your output level, how the garment is layered, and how well the finish has been maintained.
  • Garment weights are measured on a men's medium and vary by roughly 5% across the size range.
  • A waterproof rating is not a promise that you will stay dry indefinitely. Every membrane has limits, and sustained heavy rain over several hours will eventually defeat any breathable fabric.
  • Water repellent finishes wear off with use and need reactivating or reproofing. Care instructions on each product page explain how.

We will send the underlying mill test report for any figure on this site to anyone who asks. If we cannot document a number, we remove it.

Sizing and fit information

Our size guide lists body measurements in inches. Bodies vary in ways a size chart cannot capture, and our garments are cut on a unisex block, which fits a wide range of people adequately and fewer people perfectly. Size guidance is advice, not a guarantee of fit, which is why we offer a free size exchange within 30 days.

Training and health content

Articles in our journal describe how our coaches and wear testers structure training. They are general information, not medical advice, and they are not personalized to you.

  • Nothing on this site is intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
  • Consult a physician or qualified health professional before beginning or substantially changing a training program, particularly if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or are returning from injury.
  • Training carries inherent risk of injury. You are responsible for your own decisions about intensity, terrain, weather and traffic.
  • Advice about visibility, cold weather and hydration is general guidance and does not replace judgment about the specific conditions you are in.

Environmental and materials claims

Statements about recycled content, certifications and sourcing on the sustainability page reflect certificates and audit documents held at the time of publication. Certifications are renewed periodically and supply chains change. We update the page when they do, and we deliberately do not use unregulated terms such as eco-friendly, green or sustainable as product descriptors. Where we do not have data, we say so rather than estimating.

Advertising and affiliate disclosure

This site displays advertising in clearly labeled slots that are visually separated from our own content and never placed above the primary content of a page. Advertising is served by third-party networks including Google, and the presence of an advertisement is not an endorsement by Splitsec Athletic of the advertised product or service.

We do not currently use affiliate links. Every product link on this site points to our own products and we receive the full sale, not a commission from a third party. If that ever changes, we will disclose it in accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides, both here and at the point of use, in plain language next to the link itself rather than only in a footer.

We do not accept payment for editorial coverage in the journal, and we do not pay commissions to individuals for referring sales. Our referral program credits customers who refer friends, and its full terms are published on the gift cards and referrals page.

Third-party links and content

Where we link to an external site, such as an industry opt-out page or a certification body, we do not control that site and are not responsible for its content, its accuracy or its privacy practices. Links are provided because they are useful, not as an endorsement.

Photography

Campaign and editorial photography shows the products in real conditions. Images are corrected for exposure and color only. We do not retouch bodies and we do not pin or clip garments to alter how they fit. Because monitors differ, colors shown on screen are an approximation, and if a color is important to you, email us and we will describe it or send a fabric swatch.

Accuracy and availability

We try to keep prices, stock status, specifications and delivery estimates accurate, but errors happen. Where a clear error affects an order you have placed, we will contact you before charging you and offer to cancel or proceed at the correct price, as described in our Terms of Service. We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability of this website.
