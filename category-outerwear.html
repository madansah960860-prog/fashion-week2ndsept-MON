Velocity Shell Jacket

$268

Our lightest fully waterproof shell. Built for hard rain and cold wind on the move, with a hood that turns with your head and a hem that stays put over a race belt.

Fabric

  • Shell: 100% recycled ripstop nylon, 40 denier
  • Membrane: PFAS-free polyurethane, 20,000mm waterproof, 15,000g breathability
  • Weight: 8.4 oz in men's medium

Fit and care

  • Fit: Athletic regular, dropped back hem 2 in longer than front
  • Model: 6 ft 1 in, 40 in chest, wears size M
  • Care: Machine wash cold, tumble low 20 minutes to restore the finish, no softener
  • Sizing: XS to XXL, true to size over a base layer

Slipstream Wind Vest

$148

The layer you carry when the forecast is uncertain. A windproof front panel blocks the chill on descents while the mesh back lets a working engine vent. Packs into its own hand pocket.

Fabric

  • Front: 100% recycled polyester, 20 denier, windproof coating
  • Back: 88% recycled polyester, 12% elastane open mesh
  • Weight: 3.1 oz in men's medium
  • Finish: PFAS-free durable water repellent, sheds light drizzle only

Fit

  • Cut: Close, sits over a single base layer without flapping
  • Hem: Elastic gripper hem, straight cut
  • Model: 5 ft 8 in, 34 in chest, wears size S

Care and sizing

  • Wash: Cold gentle cycle, zip closed, wash with like fabrics
  • Dry: Line dry, no tumble, no iron
  • Sizing: XS to XXL. Size up if you plan to wear it over insulation

Draft Line Insulated Jacket

$312

Body-mapped synthetic insulation: 80 gram fill through the chest and kidneys, 40 gram under the arms and along the sides so you do not overheat the moment you start working.

Fabric

  • Shell: 100% recycled polyester ripstop, 30 denier
  • Insulation: 100% recycled polyester, 80 gsm core and 40 gsm side panels
  • Lining: 100% recycled polyester taffeta
  • Weight: 14.2 oz in men's medium

Fit

  • Cut: Regular with room for a mid layer
  • Sleeve: Articulated, elasticated cuff, no velcro to catch a watch
  • Model: 5 ft 11 in, 38 in chest, wears size M

Care and sizing

  • Wash: Machine wash warm, two tennis balls in the dryer keeps the fill even
  • Dry: Tumble low until fully dry, damp insulation clumps
  • Sizing: XS to XXL, true to size. Between sizes, take the larger for winter layering

Rainshadow 2.5L Jacket

$295

Built for the Pacific Northwest winter: a longer body, a wired brim on the hood and pit zips that actually open one-handed. Heavier than the Velocity Shell and warmer for it.

Fabric

  • Shell: 100% recycled polyester, 50 denier, 2.5 layer construction
  • Membrane: PFAS-free, 20,000mm waterproof, 20,000g breathability
  • Seams: Fully taped, 13mm tape
  • Weight: 12.7 oz in men's medium

Fit

  • Cut: Regular, longer body for commuting and standing around at races
  • Venting: Two-way pit zips
  • Model: 5 ft 6 in, 36 in chest, wears size S

Care and sizing

  • Wash: Cold, technical wash, close all zips first
  • Dry: Tumble low 20 minutes to reactivate the repellent finish
  • Reproof: Spray-on treatment once a season if water stops beading
  • Sizing: XS to XXL, true to size
