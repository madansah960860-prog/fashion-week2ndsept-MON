Wool care, briefly

Merino does not need washing after every session. Air it overnight and the odor goes, because wool fiber traps and releases it rather than holding bacteria the way synthetic does. When it does need a wash, cold and flat-dried keeps the fibers from felting. Our layering article covers when to reach for wool over fleece.
