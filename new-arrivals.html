How our drops work

A drop is a small, dated run of pieces that sit outside the core line. We produce a fixed quantity, publish the date in advance, and then stop. Sizes sell through at different speeds, so a drop is usually partly available for months rather than gone in an hour.

We do not run early access tiers, waitlists that are really queues, or countdown timers. The date is the date. If you miss it, most drop fabrics eventually make their way into a core style anyway.
