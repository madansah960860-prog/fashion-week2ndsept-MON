Where this started

Splitsec began with a spreadsheet of failures.

In 2018 our founders were coaching a masters running group in Southeast Portland and kept hearing the same complaints, always about the same four failure points: zip garages that shredded chins, underarm seams that split, waistband elastic that gave out after a season, and screen prints that cracked by March. The garments were not cheap. They simply were not built for people training five days a week in wet weather.

So the spreadsheet got started: every garment failure reported by the group, what failed, how many sessions it had done, and what it cost. After eighteen months there were four hundred rows. The patterns were obvious. Failures clustered in construction, not fabric. Almost nobody wore out a fabric. They wore out a seam, a zip, or a trim.

Splitsec Athletic incorporated in Portland in 2019 with one product, the piece that became the Thermal Grid Crew, and one rule that still holds: construction decisions come before fabric decisions, and fabric decisions come before anything that looks good in a photograph.

We would rather ship thirty styles that hold up than a hundred that photograph well.

How a garment gets made here

Every piece goes through the same five stages, and the process is deliberately slow. From first sketch to a product page usually takes between eleven and sixteen months.

  1. Failure brief

    We start from the complaint, not the concept. Every new piece begins with a written brief describing what fails on the existing options and how we will avoid it.

  2. Fabric selection and lab testing

    Candidate fabrics are tested for abrasion, pilling, colorfastness and dimensional stability after fifty wash cycles. Anything that shifts more than 3% in either direction is rejected.

  3. Pattern and first sample

    Patterns are graded across the full XS to XXL range, not scaled from a single midpoint. Grading from one size is why so much apparel fits badly at the extremes.

  4. Eight-week wear test

    Twelve athletes across mixed climates wear the sample for eight weeks and log every session. Failures stop the run. This has killed four products outright since 2019.

  5. Production and pricing

    We produce in small runs with two partner factories, publish the composition and weight, and set the price on landed cost plus a fixed margin. No fake anchor prices, no permanent discount cycle.

The business model, in plain terms

We sell direct to you, in US dollars, from our own website. That is the entire revenue model, plus a small wholesale channel and gift cards.

Direct to consumer

  • Every retail order is placed on this site and shipped by us from Portland
  • Prices are set once and held. There is no seasonal markdown calendar
  • Because there is no wholesale margin to protect, a $268 shell is priced on what it costs to make, not on a department store's markup structure
  • We do not run flash sales, and we do not raise a price in order to discount it later

Seasonless core plus dated drops

  • Twenty-two of our thirty styles are core and stay in stock all year
  • Core styles get restocked, not replaced, usually every six to eight weeks
  • Two capsule drops a year. Drop 06 released March 6, 2026, Drop 07 opens October 2, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Pacific
  • Drops are produced in a fixed quantity and are not restocked once sold through

Bench Made, our made-to-order line

  • Cut and sewn in Portland after the order is placed
  • Current lead time is 18 to 24 business days, published on each product page
  • Payment is taken at order because fabric is cut for you specifically
  • Exchangeable for another size within 30 days, not eligible for refund

Wholesale and stockists

  • A small number of independent run and training shops carry the core line
  • Opening order minimum, margin and lead times are published rather than negotiated case by case
  • We do not sell to marketplaces or third-party resale platforms
  • Apply through the stockist page

Shipping and returns economics

  • Free US standard shipping over $125, flat $8 below it. The threshold covers our actual average parcel cost
  • 30 days to return or exchange, unworn with tags
  • Exchanges: we pay shipping both ways, once per order
  • Refund returns: a $7 label fee is deducted, because someone has to pay for the freight and we would rather charge the returner than raise every price

Gift cards and referrals

  • Digital gift cards from $25 to $500, no expiry date and no dormancy fees
  • Referral program: your friend gets $20 off their first order, you get $20 once it ships
  • No paid influencer commissions on individual sales
  • Details on the gift cards page

The company, honestly sized

Splitsec is fourteen people. Four in design and development, three in operations and fulfillment, two in customer support, two on the Bench Made line, and three across finance, marketing and everything else. We are not a large company pretending to be small, and we are not a small company pretending to be large.

Everything ships from our own space on SE Water Avenue. When you email support, the reply comes from someone at that address who can walk twenty feet and look at the item you are asking about.

Things we do not do

  • No fake scarcity. No countdown timers, no "only two left" banners. Stock status is either available or sold through
  • No invented reviews. We do not publish testimonials we wrote ourselves or star ratings we cannot verify
  • No performance claims we cannot show. If we say 20,000mm, that is a lab number from the mill, and we will send you the test report if you ask
  • No body-shape language. Our copy describes garments, not the bodies wearing them
  • No dark patterns. Unsubscribing takes one click, the cookie banner has a real reject option, and nothing auto-adds to your bag

These are not marketing positions. They are operating constraints that cost us money, and we accept the cost because the alternative is a business we would not want to run.
