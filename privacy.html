1. Who we are

Splitsec Athletic, Inc. is a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1420 SE Water Avenue, Suite 210, Portland, OR 97214, United States. We operate the website at splitsec.com and sell performance apparel directly to consumers. For the purposes of this policy, Splitsec Athletic, Inc. is the controller of the personal information described below.

You can reach our privacy contact at privacy@splitsec.com or by writing to the address above, marked for the attention of Privacy.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Order information: name, shipping address, billing address, email address, phone number where you provide one, and the contents of your order.
  • Payment information: your card details are entered directly into our payment processor's hosted fields. We receive a token, the last four digits, the card brand and the authorization result. We never receive or store your full card number or security code.
  • Account information if you create one: email address, a hashed password and your order history.
  • Support correspondence: the content of emails, contact form messages, return requests and wholesale applications, including anything you choose to put in them.
  • Marketing preferences: your email address if you subscribe, and whether you have opened or clicked our emails.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and connection data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, screen size and referring page.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, time on page, links clicked and search terms used on our site.
  • Cookie identifiers as described in section 4 and in the Cookie Policy.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise geolocation.
  • We do not collect biometric data, health data or government identifiers.
  • We do not buy customer lists or append third-party demographic data to your record.

3. How we use it

  • To fulfil orders: processing payment, shipping goods, sending confirmations and handling returns, exchanges and repairs.
  • To provide support: answering your questions and keeping a record of the conversation so you do not have to repeat yourself.
  • To operate the site: security, fraud prevention, load balancing and debugging.
  • To improve what we sell: aggregated analysis of which pages help and which products are returned, and why.
  • To send marketing email, only if you have opted in, and only until you opt out.
  • To measure advertising, only where you have consented to advertising cookies.
  • To meet legal obligations: tax records, accounting and responding to lawful requests.

We do not use your personal information to train machine learning models, and we do not sell it.

4. Cookies and tracking

A cookie is a small file stored by your browser. We use three categories, and nothing outside the first category is set until you make a choice in the consent banner.

  • Strictly necessary. Required for the site to work: keeping your bag contents, remembering your cookie choice, session security and fraud prevention. These cannot be switched off, and they do not track you across other websites.
  • Analytics. Aggregated measurement of traffic and page performance, so we know which pages help. Set only with your consent.
  • Advertising. Used to measure and personalize advertising, including cookies set by Google. Set only with your consent.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Preferences link, which appears in the footer of every page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent the bag from working.

We do not respond to browser Do Not Track signals, because there is no consistent industry standard for how to interpret them. We do honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of sale or sharing where applicable law requires it.

The full list of cookies, their purpose and their duration is in the Cookie Policy.

5. Google and advertising

Where you have consented to advertising cookies, we work with Google as an advertising vendor. The following applies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • You may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at optout.aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy and at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you reject advertising cookies in our banner, no advertising cookie is set by us or by Google through this site, and no advertising identifier is transmitted.

6. Who we share data with

We share the minimum necessary with service providers who are contractually bound to use it only to provide their service to us. Categories, and what each receives:

  • Payment processing: name, billing address, order value and card token.
  • Shipping carriers: name, shipping address, phone number where required for delivery, and parcel weight.
  • Email service provider: email address, first name and subscription status, for those who opt in.
  • Analytics provider: pseudonymous usage data, where you have consented.
  • Advertising vendors including Google: cookie identifiers and conversion events, where you have consented.
  • Customer support tooling: your correspondence and order reference.
  • Accountants and auditors: transaction records as required for tax and financial reporting.

We may also disclose information where legally required, in response to valid legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our customers, our staff or the public. If Splitsec were acquired or merged, customer data would transfer as a business asset, and we would notify you by email before that took effect.

We do not sell your personal information for money. See section 10 for what "sale" and "sharing" mean under California law, which uses a broader definition than everyday usage.

7. How long we keep it

  • Order and transaction records: seven years, to meet US tax and accounting requirements.
  • Support correspondence: three years from the last message in the thread.
  • Marketing subscriber records: until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record kept indefinitely so we do not email you again by mistake.
  • Account data: until you ask us to delete the account, subject to the order retention above.
  • Analytics data: 14 months, then deleted or aggregated beyond identification.
  • Server logs: 90 days.
  • Cookie consent records: 12 months, after which we ask you again.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS with TLS. Payment card data is handled entirely by our PCI DSS compliant payment processor and does not pass through our servers. Access to customer records inside the company is limited to staff who need it for their role, is logged, and requires multi-factor authentication. Passwords, where accounts exist, are stored hashed and salted.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable state and federal law, without unreasonable delay.

9. Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact privacy@splitsec.com and we will delete it promptly. We also do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16, as those terms are used under California law.

10. California rights, CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights:

  • Right to know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the sources, the business purpose, and the categories of third parties we disclose it to.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions such as completing a transaction or meeting tax obligations.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights. We will not deny you goods, charge you a different price or give you a lower level of service.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. However, when advertising cookies are enabled with your consent, the transfer of cookie identifiers to advertising vendors including Google may qualify as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law.

To opt out, do any one of the following. All three achieve the same result.

  • Choose Reject non-essential in our cookie banner, or open Cookie Preferences and switch Advertising off.
  • Enable a Global Privacy Control signal in your browser. We honor it automatically.
  • Email privacy@splitsec.com with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share" and we will apply the opt-out to your customer record.

To exercise any other right, email privacy@splitsec.com or call (503) 555-0142. We will verify your identity by asking you to confirm details from a recent order, or by sending a confirmation to the email address on file. We respond within 45 days and may extend once by a further 45 days where necessary, telling you if we do.

You may use an authorized agent to submit a request. We will ask for written proof of authorization and, in most cases, will still verify your identity directly.

11. Other US state rights

Residents of Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have broadly similar rights: to access, correct, delete and obtain a portable copy of their personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Use the same contact route in section 14 and tell us which state you reside in.

Where a state law provides an appeal process for a denied request, you may appeal by replying to our decision email. We will respond to appeals within 45 days and will explain how to contact your state attorney general if you remain dissatisfied.

12. Note for EU and UK visitors

We ship to the European Union and the United Kingdom, so this section applies if you are located there. Our legal bases for processing are:

  • Performance of a contract for order fulfilment, payment and returns.
  • Consent for marketing email and for analytics and advertising cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests for site security, fraud prevention and basic service improvement, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation for tax and accounting records.

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Personal data is transferred to the United States, where our systems are located, under Standard Contractual Clauses with our processors. You may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority, or with the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will email registered customers before it takes effect. Prior versions are available on request.
