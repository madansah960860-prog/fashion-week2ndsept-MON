Size guide

Every measurement on this page is a body measurement in inches, not a garment measurement. Measure yourself, find the row, and read the fit note for the garment you are looking at. If you are on a line between two sizes, the advice below is specific rather than generic.

How to measure

  1. Chest

    Tape around the fullest part of the chest, under the arms, with the tape level across the back. Arms down, breathe out normally.

  2. Natural waist

    The narrowest part of the torso, usually an inch above the navel. Do not pull the tape tight and do not hold your breath.

  3. Hip

    Around the fullest part of the seat, feet together.

  4. Inseam

    From the crotch seam of a pair of pants that fit you, down the inside leg to the hem.

  5. Head

    Around the widest part, roughly one finger width above the eyebrows and over the ears.

Fit notes by top

  • Velocity Shell, Rainshadow, Nightsplit: Athletic regular. Sized to fit a base layer plus a mid layer
  • Crosswind Anorak: Relaxed. Size down if you want a trimmer line
  • Thermal Grid Crew, Interval Waffle: Slim, designed to sit close under a shell
  • Marginal Gains Hoodie, Loop Terry: Relaxed with a dropped shoulder. Size down for a standard fit
  • Sprint Singlet: Race fit. Size up for training rather than racing

Between two sizes

Take the smaller size when

  • You are buying a racing piece and want it close to the body
  • The garment is listed as relaxed or dropped shoulder
  • You are between sizes on chest but at the lower end on waist
  • The fabric contains 8% or more elastane, which recovers well

Take the larger size when

  • You will wear a mid layer underneath a shell
  • You are at the top of the chest range and prefer a full range of motion overhead
  • The garment is a woven with no stretch, such as the Crosswind Anorak
  • You prefer knitwear that skims rather than holds

Still not sure

Send us your chest, waist and height and the piece you are considering. We answer sizing email within one business day and we will tell you if we think a piece will not work for you, including when the honest answer is that another brand fits your proportions better.
