Returns and exchanges

Thirty days from delivery, unworn with tags attached. Size exchanges ship free both ways. Refund returns carry a $7 label fee. That is the whole policy, and the detail below explains how it works and why it is set up this way.

We charge the $7 label fee on refund returns because the freight is a real cost that has to sit somewhere. Building it into every price would mean customers who keep their orders subsidise those who do not. Charging it at the point of return is the fairer arrangement, and it is why our prices are not padded.

How to start a return

  1. Send the request

    Use the form on this page or email help@splitsec.com with your order number, the item, and whether you want an exchange or a refund. No account or login is required.

  2. We email a prepaid label

    Usually within a few hours, always within one business day. The label is a UPS or USPS return label you print at home, or a QR code you can show at a drop-off point without printing anything.

  3. Pack it and drop it off

    Reuse our mailer if it survived. Tags need to be attached and the item needs to be unworn and unwashed. There is no return form to fill in and put in the box.

  4. We inspect and process

    Within two business days of arrival. For exchanges we ship the new size the same day we receive the old one. We hold your requested size in reserve while the return is in transit so it does not sell out from under you.

  5. Refund lands

    Refunds go back to the original payment method. We process within two business days, then your bank takes three to ten business days to post it. We email you the moment we process it.

What we can and cannot take back

Eligible

  • Anything unworn and unwashed with tags attached, within 30 days of delivery
  • Items tried on indoors. Obviously you have to put it on to know
  • Gift purchases, returned by the recipient for store credit at the price paid
  • Items bought with referral credit, refunded to the original payment split
  • Faulty items at any point, with no time limit, under our repair and warranty terms

Not eligible

  • Items worn outdoors or washed, because we cannot resell them
  • Socks and headbands once the hygiene seal or packaging is opened
  • Bench Made made-to-order pieces for refund. Size exchange only
  • Gift cards, which are non-refundable but never expire
  • Items from our resale program, which are sold as final sale and clearly labeled

Faulty items and warranty

  • Manufacturing faults are repaired or replaced at no cost, with no time limit
  • A fault means a failure in materials or construction, such as a seam that opens or a zip that fails under normal use
  • Wear and tear is not a fault, but we will quote a repair, typically $18 to $45
  • Send a photograph first. We quote before you post anything

If we made the mistake

  • Wrong size, wrong item, missing item or damaged in transit
  • We pay shipping both ways and no fee applies
  • Replacement ships before the original comes back
  • No photographs required, and no claim form

Try two sizes if you are unsure

Ordering two sizes and returning one is a legitimate use of this policy and we would rather you did that than guess wrong. Free US shipping applies over $125, and one size exchange per order is free both ways. If you keep neither, the $7 label fee applies once, not twice.
