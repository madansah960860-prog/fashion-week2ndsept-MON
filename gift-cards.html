Gift cards and referrals

Two ways to give someone Splitsec without guessing their size. Both have terms short enough to read, and neither has an expiry date buried in a footnote.

Digital gift cards

Delivered by email, usually within a minute, with an option to schedule delivery for a specific date. Redeemable against anything on the site including Bench Made made-to-order pieces.

Amounts and terms

  • Amounts: $25, $50, $100, $150, $250 or $500
  • Expiry: None. Gift cards do not expire and carry no dormancy or inactivity fees
  • Balance: Partial use is fine, the remaining balance stays on the card
  • Combining: Can be combined with referral credit and with each other
  • Refunds: Gift cards themselves are non-refundable. Items bought with one follow the standard 30-day return policy
  • Lost card: Email us with the purchase details and we will reissue it

We send the payment link here and confirm when the card is delivered.

Referral credit

Your friend gets $20 off their first order. You get $20 in credit once that order ships and clears the return window.

How it works

  • Share your referral code with someone who has not ordered from us before
  • They enter it at checkout and $20 comes off an order of $100 or more
  • Once their order ships and the 30-day return window closes, $20 in credit lands in your account
  • Credit is applied automatically at your next checkout

The rules, in full

  • New customers only, one referral discount per person
  • Minimum order of $100 for the friend's discount to apply
  • No limit on how many people you refer
  • Credit expires 12 months after it is issued, which we will remind you about by email
  • Cannot be exchanged for cash
  • We may withhold credit where we see clear self-referral, and we will explain why rather than silently voiding it

What this is not

  • Not an affiliate program. There are no commissions on sales and no tiers
  • Not a paid influencer arrangement
  • No tracking pixels are placed on your friend by the referral link
  • We do not email people you refer unless they subscribe themselves
