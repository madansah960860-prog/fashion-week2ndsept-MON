What to expect when you write

Sizing questions

  • Send chest, waist and height in inches and the style you are considering
  • We will name a size and tell you what to expect at the shoulder and hem
  • If we think the piece will not suit your proportions, we will say so

Order problems

  • Include the order number from your confirmation email
  • Missing or damaged parcels are resolved by us, not passed to the carrier
  • We do not require photographs before starting a replacement

Repairs and warranty

  • Manufacturing faults are repaired or replaced at no cost, with no time limit
  • Wear and tear repairs are charged at parts cost plus return shipping
  • Send a photo of the damage and we will quote before you post anything
