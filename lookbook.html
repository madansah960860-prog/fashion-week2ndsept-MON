Drop 06 campaign

Crosswind

Four mornings in March, three Portland run clubs, and whatever weather showed up. Nothing here was shot in a studio.

Shop Drop 06 Read the journal

The brief was simple

Photograph the clothes being used, in the weather they were designed for, on people who train four or more times a week. No stylists, no borrowed shoes, no fake sweat. The athletes wore their own kit alongside ours and kept their own routes.

We shot at 6:10 a.m. on four consecutive mornings so the light was consistent. Two mornings were wet, one was cold and clear, and one was fog thick enough that the fourth set is mostly silhouette. We kept those frames because that is what a Portland March looks like.

Campaign credits

  • Location: Southeast Portland, Oregon, and the Springwater Corridor
  • Dates: March 3 to March 6, 2026
  • Athletes: Members of three Portland run clubs, paid at a day rate
  • Pieces shown: Crosswind Anorak, Session Tee, Split Tights, Velocity Shell, Splitline Cap
  • Retouching: Exposure and color only. No body reshaping, no garment fixing

What we will not do in a campaign

  • No body retouching. We correct exposure and color balance. We do not reshape anyone
  • No pinned garments. If a piece needs a clip at the back to look right, the pattern is wrong
  • No borrowed athletes. Everyone photographed trains regularly and was paid a day rate
  • No weather faking. Wet frames were shot in rain. The fog is fog
  • No invented testimonials. If we quote someone, they said it and approved it

That last point matters more than it sounds. It is easy to write a line of praise and attribute it to an athlete. We would rather show the clothes working and let you decide.
