Frequently asked questions

These are the twenty questions our support inbox gets most, answered in full rather than deflected to a policy page. If yours is not here, email us and we will answer it and then add it.

Sizing and fit

XS through XXL across every apparel style, with US numeric equivalents listed in the size guide. Socks come in S, M and L mapped to US shoe sizes. Gloves run S to XL. Caps, beanies and headbands are one size with a stated fit range in inches.

We grade patterns across the full range rather than scaling from a single midpoint, which is why the fit at XS and XXL is closer to intended than it is with many brands.

It depends on the garment rather than on you. Take the smaller size for race-fit pieces and for anything with 8% or more elastane. Take the larger size for non-stretch wovens like the Crosswind Anorak, and whenever you will wear a mid layer underneath.

The size guide has a specific note for every style. If you would rather ask, send your chest, waist and height to our team and we will name a size.

Body measurements, in inches. Garment measurements are available on request for any style if you want to compare against something you already own. Email us the style and we will send the flat measurements for that size.

Unisex, cut on a straight block with articulated sleeves and a hip allowance built into the pattern rather than a separate size run. We are direct about the trade-off: a unisex cut fits a wider range of people adequately and fewer people perfectly. If you have significantly different chest and hip proportions, email us before ordering.

Synthetics do not shrink. Cotton and cotton-blend pieces can lose up to 3% in length on the first wash, and our patterns already allow for that. Merino will shrink and felt if washed warm, so cold wool cycle only and lay it flat to dry. Each product page states the expected behaviour.

Ordering and shipping

Free standard shipping on orders over $125. A flat $8 below that. Expedited is $18 flat regardless of order value. Alaska, Hawaii and US territories ship USPS Priority in three to seven business days. Full detail on the shipping page.

Within one business day of the order, from our own warehouse in Portland. Orders placed after 1:00 p.m. Pacific on a Friday go out on Monday. You get a tracking number by email as soon as the label is created.

To Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia and New Zealand. Rates are calculated at checkout by weight and destination. Duties and import taxes are paid by the recipient on delivery, and we cannot estimate them for you because they vary by country and by the classification your customs authority applies.

Yes, if it has not shipped. Email us with the order number and we will change the size, the address or cancel it outright. Once a tracking number exists it is with the carrier, and the fastest route is to accept delivery and start a return.

Tell us and we will resolve it rather than sending you to the carrier. In our experience most of these appear within 48 hours, often with a neighbour or at a side entrance. If it has not appeared after two business days we send a replacement and take the carrier claim on ourselves.

Returns, exchanges and repairs

Thirty days from delivery, unworn, unwashed and with tags attached. Start it from the returns page with your order number and email address.

Size exchanges: we do, both ways, once per order. Refund returns: a $7 label fee is deducted from your refund. Faulty items or anything shipped in error: we pay, always, and there is no fee.

We charge for refund returns because the freight has to be paid by someone. The alternative is building it into every price, which penalises people who keep what they buy.

You can exchange it for a different size within 30 days, but Bench Made pieces are not eligible for a refund, because fabric was cut specifically for your order. This is stated on the product page before you buy, and you can change the size within 48 hours of ordering at no cost.

Manufacturing faults are repaired or replaced free with no time limit. Wear and tear repairs, including zip replacement and patching, are charged at parts cost plus return shipping, typically $18 to $45, with a two to three week turnaround from our Portland workshop. Send a photo first and we will quote before you post anything.

We process refunds within two business days of the return arriving. Your bank then takes three to ten business days to post it, which is outside our control. We email you when we process it, so you have a timestamp if you need to chase your card issuer.

Products, care and the company

No seasonal sale calendar, no flash sales and no discount codes as a routine acquisition tool. Prices are set on landed cost plus a fixed margin and held. We do sell repaired and returned items at a reduced price through our resale program, and we do run a referral credit, which is the closest thing to a discount we operate.

Drop 07 opens on October 2, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Pacific. Drop 06 released on March 6, 2026 and is still available in most sizes. We publish the date once it is confirmed and we do not run countdown timers or early access tiers.

Cold, gentle, inside out, and never with fabric softener. Softener coats fibers and destroys both wicking and water repellency. For waterproof shells, a technical wash plus twenty minutes on low tumble restores the repellent finish. For merino, cold wool cycle and lay flat.

Every product page carries specific instructions for that fabric, and they are worth following rather than generalising.

Gift cards never expire and carry no fees, from $25 to $500, delivered by email. The referral program gives your friend $20 off their first order and gives you $20 in credit once that order ships. Full terms on the gift cards page.
